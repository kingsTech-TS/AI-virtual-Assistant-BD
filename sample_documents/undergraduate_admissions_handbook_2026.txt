1. GENERAL ADMISSION PHILOSOPHY & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Admissions Board of Apex Federal University operates an open, merit-driven, 
and transparent admissions process designed to select qualified candidates across 
diverse academic backgrounds into undergraduate degree programs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GENERAL UNDERGRADUATE ENTRY REQUIREMENTS (100-LEVEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Candidates seeking admission into 100-Level undergraduate degree programs must 
satisfy the following minimum criteria:
1. Minimum Age: Candidates must be at least 16 years of age by October 31 of 
   the admission year.
2. O-Level Qualifications: A minimum of five (5) credit passes in relevant 
   Senior Secondary Certificate Examination (SSCE / WAEC / NECO / NABTEB) subjects 
   obtained in NOT more than two (2) sittings.
3. Compulsory Core Subjects: English Language and Mathematics are mandatory credit 
   passes for all degree courses across all faculties.
4. UTME Score: A minimum score of 200 in the Unified Tertiary Matriculation 
   Examination (UTME) for science and technology disciplines, and at least 180 
   for arts, management, and social sciences.
5. First Choice Institution: The University must be selected as the candidate's 
   First Choice institution on the JAMB portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FACULTY & DEPARTMENT-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS (O-LEVEL & UTME COMBINATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Faculty of Physical Sciences
- Department of Computer Science (B.Sc. Computer Science):
  * O-Level Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and any 
    other Science subject (Biology, Further Mathematics, or Computer Studies).
  * UTME Subject Combination: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.
  * Departmental Cut-Off Point: 220 in UTME + 65% in Post-UTME Screening.

- Department of Physics (B.Sc. Physics / Applied Geophysics):
  * O-Level Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and 
    Biology or Further Mathematics.
  * UTME Subject Combination: English Language, Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry.
  * Departmental Cut-Off Point: 200 in UTME.

- Department of Mathematics & Statistics (B.Sc. Mathematics):
  * O-Level Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, and two other 
    science subjects.
  * UTME Subject Combination: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.

- Department of Chemistry (B.Sc. Industrial Chemistry):
  * O-Level Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, and Biology.
  * UTME Subject Combination: English Language, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics/Biology.

B. Faculty of Engineering & Technology
- Electrical / Mechanical / Civil / Software Engineering:
  * O-Level: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Further 
    Mathematics or Technical Drawing.
  * UTME: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (Minimum UTME: 230).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DIRECT ENTRY (200-LEVEL) ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Candidates with advanced qualifications may apply for Direct Entry into 200-Level:
1. Cambridge GCE / IJMB / JUPEB: Minimum of 10 points across three (3) relevant 
   Advanced Level subjects.
2. National Diploma (ND): Upper Credit (minimum CGPA 3.00/4.00) from an accredited 
   polytechnic or monotechnic in a relevant field.
3. Higher National Diploma (HND): Upper Credit holders may be considered for 
   200-Level or 300-Level depending on faculty curriculum alignment.
4. Professional Diplomas or certificates without accredited O-Level prerequisites 
   are strictly NOT accepted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STEP-BY-STEP APPLICATION & POST-UTME SCREENING PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Online Portal Registration
- Visit the official university admissions portal (admissions.afuts.edu.ng).
- Enter your UTME Registration Number to verify eligibility.
- Pay the non-refundable Post-UTME screening fee of N2,000 via the integrated 
  online payment gateway.

Step 2: Bio-Data & Credential Upload
- Fill out the digital screening form with personal details, O-Level grades, 
  and examination center details.
- Upload clear scanned copies of:
  * O-Level statement of results / original certificates (JPEG/PDF, max 500KB).
  * UTME result slip showing candidate passport photograph.
  * Birth Certificate or statutory declaration of age.
  * Local Government Identification Certificate.

Step 3: Post-UTME Computer-Based Screening Test (CBT)
- Print the Screening Registration Slip containing your assigned CBT examination 
  date, venue, and time.
- Arrive at the University ICT Examination Center 45 minutes before test commencement.
- Prohibited items: Mobile phones, smart watches, electronic calculators, and 
  unauthorized materials.

6. ADMISSION NOTIFICATION, ACCEPTANCE & CLEARANCE GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Checking Admission Status:
   - Admitted candidates will receive an SMS and email notification. Status can 
     also be verified on JAMB CAPS and the AFUTS admissions portal.
2. JAMB CAPS Acceptance:
   - Candidates must click 'ACCEPT ADMISSION' on JAMB Central Admissions 
     Processing System (CAPS) within four (4) weeks of offer.
3. Non-Refundable Acceptance Fee:
   - Pay the mandatory Acceptance Fee of N35,000 on the student portal within 
     two (2) weeks of offer to avoid automatic forfeiture.
4. Departmental Verification & Physical Clearance:
   - Present original credentials at the Departmental Admissions Office for 
     physical screening.
   - Collect the official Admission Verification Certificate and matriculation docket.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DEFERMENT OF ADMISSION POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Newly admitted students who are unable to resume for verifiable medical, 
  financial, or compassionate reasons may apply for Deferment of Admission for 
  one (1) academic session.
- Requirements for Deferment:
  * Payment of the non-refundable Acceptance Fee.
  * Formal written application addressed to the Registrar through the Head of Department.
  * Deferment processing fee of N10,000.
  * Submission deadline is six (6) weeks after semester resumption.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. FRAUD, FORGERY & DISQUALIFICATION CLAUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The University maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards academic dishonesty and 
forgery. Any candidate found to have submitted forged results, false bio-data, 
or manipulated credentials will face immediate disqualification, withdrawal of 
admission, and prosecution by law enforcement agencies.
